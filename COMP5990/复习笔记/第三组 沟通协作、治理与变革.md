## Part 1 — Professional communication（W4）

### 为什么沟通关键 `★★★`

> [!tip] Practice Q5：系统技术达标但用户不满意——核心往往是沟通与反馈链断了。

**中文**

- Communication 是 IT projects 的 **backbone**
- 沟通保证 developers/testers/PM 与 clients/users/external stakeholders **alignment**
- Poor communication → delays, cost overruns, project failure
- Communication in IT 的关键用途：requirements gathering、team coordination、stakeholder engagement、reporting progress
- 课件总结：Success = **Technology + Communication**

**Term**

| English | 中文 |
|--------|------|
| backbone | 主干/支柱 |
| alignment | 对齐（目标/理解） |
| requirement gathering | 需求收集 |
| stakeholder engagement | 利益相关方参与 |
| reporting progress | 进度汇报 |

**Useful phrase**

- *Communication is the **backbone** of IT projects.*  
- *Poor communication leads to **delays**, **cost overruns**, and **project failure**.*  
- *Success equals **technology plus communication**.*

---

### Types of communication `★`

Written / Verbal / Presentation-Visual / Non-verbal

（考试更常问框架与实践，不常要求背定义。）

---

### 7Cs `★★★`

**Clear / Concise / Concrete / Correct / Coherent / Complete / Courteous**

**Useful phrase**

- *The 7Cs ensure messages are clear, accurate, and actionable, reducing misunderstandings and rework.*

---

### SBAR `★★★`

> [!tip] Practice Q6b：用 SBAR 给高层做 progress update。

| 步 | 写什么 |
|----|--------|
| **Situation** | 现在发生了什么（短、直接） |
| **Background** | 背景与重要性 |
| **Assessment** | 你的分析：原因/影响 |
| **Recommendation** | 下一步行动 + 你需要对方做什么 |

**Term**：concise, evidence-based update / escalation / recommendation

**Useful phrase**

- *SBAR provides **concise, evidence-based updates** that clarify issues and recommendations for quick decisions.*
- *Recommendation should state **what you need** from the other person.*

---

### 常见沟通障碍与改进 `★★`

**Barriers**

- jargon/acronyms
- cultural & language differences
- remote collaboration（time zones）
- assumptions + lack of active listening
- poor documentation → knowledge gaps

**Good vs bad update（课件示例总结）**

- bad：vague、no timeline、no next steps
- good：说明原因 + 给明确时点 + 承诺更新 + 必要时升级（escalate）

**Useful phrase**

- *Avoid vague updates; include the cause, timeline, next steps, and escalation path.*

## Part 2 — Teamwork（W4）

### 有效团队特征 & 常见误区 `★★`

**误区纠正**（课件）

- “Teamwork = everyone agree” → healthy disagreements improve outcomes
- “Teamwork slows down” → collaboration reduces rework/errors
- “Good teams don’t have conflicts” → conflicts are normal; resolution matters
- “Equal contribution” → roles contribute differently

**Effective team characteristics**

- clear roles & responsibilities
- trust & psychological safety
- open, respectful communication
- shared goals & accountability
- adapt to change
- balance technical + soft skills

**Term**：psychological safety / shared accountability / adapt to change

---

### 心理安全 Psychological safety `★★★`

**定义（课件）**：team members can take risks without fear of embarrassment/punishment。

**为什么重要（IT）**

- encourages speaking up about errors → fewer failures
- promotes innovation/creativity
- improves engagement, morale, resilience

**建立方法（课件）**

- encourage curiosity
- normalize mistakes（errors as learning）
- active listening without judgement
- inclusive environment
- clear expectations

**Useful phrase**

- *Psychological safety is the shared belief that team members can take risks without fear of embarrassment or punishment.*
- *It reduces fear of speaking up about errors, leading to fewer failures.*

---

### Tuckman stages `★★`

课件引用：Tuckman 团队发展阶段（复习时对照课件图）。常用于判断团队处于哪一阶段并解释现象（如 storming）。

**Useful phrase**

- *In the storming stage, conflicts and role clarity issues often surface before norms stabilise.*

---

### 冲突：Task vs Relationship + TKI `★★`

**Task conflict（cognitive）**：关于 goals/ideas/processes 的争论，可建设性。  
**Relationship conflict（affective）**：人格不合/不信任，通常破坏性。

**TKI**：基于 assertiveness（self）与 cooperativeness（others）的五种 conflict handling modes（课件图）。

**Term**：constructive vs destructive conflict / assertiveness / cooperativeness

---

### Agile teamwork ceremonies `★`

Daily standup / Sprint planning / Sprint review / Sprint retrospective（常作为“保证沟通与反馈”的例子）。

## Part 3 — Stakeholder management（W4）

### Stakeholder 定义与价值 `★★★`

**定义**：individuals, groups, or organisations affected by or having an interest in the project.

**为什么重要**

- aligns expectations with deliverables
- minimises conflict and scope creep
- builds trust and project credibility

**Useful phrase**

- *Stakeholder management aligns expectations with deliverables and reduces scope creep.*

---

### 分析与映射：Power–Interest + Salience `★★★`

**Power–Interest Matrix（Mendelow）**

- power：ability to influence
- interest：level of concern/involvement

**Salience model（advanced）**：power + legitimacy + urgency

- definitive（P+L+U）highest priority
- dominant（P+L）
- dependent（L+U）
- dangerous（P+U）
- dormant（P only）
- discretionary（L only）
- demanding（U only）

**Useful phrase**

- *The power–interest matrix helps decide how much time and effort to invest in managing each stakeholder.*
- *The salience model prioritises stakeholders based on power, legitimacy, and urgency.*

---

### Engagement levels（Inform→Empower） `★★`

Inform / Consult / Involve / Collaborate / Empower  
多渠道：reports, demos, townhalls, dashboards

**Useful phrase**

- *Match engagement strategies to stakeholder category and use multiple channels to maintain visibility and feedback loops.*

---

### Best practices & challenges `★★`

**Best practices**

- start early + update regularly
- use frameworks（7Cs, SBAR）
- manage expectations（under-promise, over-deliver）
- trust & transparency
- document decisions & agreements
- don’t neglect low power, high interest users
- regular engagement（reviews, demos）

**Challenges**

- changing requirements
- conflicting interests
- cultural/language barriers
- unrealistic expectations
- hidden stakeholders
- over/under-communicating

## Part 4 — IT Governance（W6）

### IT Governance 定义与三问 `★★★`

**定义**：leadership + structures + processes，确保 IT systems/strategies 支持 business goals、deliver value、manage risks。

**三问（课件原句结构）**

1. Are we doing the right things with IT? → alignment with business strategy  
2. Are we doing them the right way? → processes, accountability, compliance  
3. Are we getting the benefits? → value delivery, performance measurement

**Useful phrase**

- *IT governance ensures IT supports business goals, delivers value, and manages risk.*

---

### 治理成功与失败信号 `★★`

**Success elements**

- strategic alignment
- value delivery
- risk management
- resource optimisation
- performance measurement（KPIs, balanced scorecards, audits）

**Failure causes**

- lack of executive support
- misalignment with business goals
- poor communication/awareness
- resistance to change（seen as bureaucracy）

---

### COBIT：EDM vs PBRM（APO/BAI/DSS/MEA） `★★★`

**COBIT（ISACA）**：governance + management framework

- **Governance objectives**：**EDM**（Evaluate, Direct, Monitor）
- **Management objectives**：**PBRM**（Plan, Build, Run, Monitor）

PBRM 展开（课件）：

- Plan → **APO**（Align, Plan, Organise）
- Build → **BAI**（Build, Acquire, Implement）
- Run → **DSS**（Deliver, Service, Support）
- Monitor → **MEA**（Monitor, Evaluate, Assess）

**Useful phrase**

- *Governance sets direction (EDM), while management plans, builds, runs, and monitors (PBRM).*

---

### COBIT 原则：COBIT 5 五原则 + COBIT 2019 定制化 `★★`

**COBIT 5 五原则**

1. meeting stakeholder needs
2. covering the enterprise end-to-end
3. single integrated framework（ITIL/ISO/TOGAF）
4. enabling a holistic approach
5. separating governance from management

**COBIT 2019**：强调 tailoring/customisation（design factors）→ governance system 按 strategy/risk/size/regulation 定制。

---

### ITSM/ITIL：COBIT vs ITIL `★★★`

**ITSM**：把 IT 当作 services 来管理，关注 processes/people/services outcomes（不仅是技术）。

**COBIT vs ITIL（课件表）**

- COBIT：governance & management；alignment/control/value/risk；strategic & tactical
- ITIL：ITSM best practice；how services delivered/managed；operational & tactical

**Useful phrase**

- *COBIT defines what should be done for alignment and control, while ITIL focuses on how services should be delivered and managed.*

---

### ITIL v3 lifecycle vs ITIL 4 SVS/SVC `★★★`

**ITIL v3 Service Lifecycle**

- Service Strategy
- Service Design
- Service Transition
- Service Operation
- Continual Service Improvement

**ITIL 4**：value-driven、flexible、integrates Agile/Lean/DevOps

- **SVS** components：guiding principles / governance / service value chain / practices / continual improvement
- Guiding principles（7）：focus on value；start where you are；progress iteratively with feedback；collaborate and promote visibility；think/work holistically；keep it simple and practical；optimize and automate
- **Service Value Chain**（6）：Plan / Improve / Engage / Design & Transition / Obtain/Build / Deliver & Support

**Useful phrase**

- *ITIL 4 shifts from rigid processes to a flexible, value-driven approach focused on value co-creation.*

## Part 5 — Organisational Change Management（W7）

### Project management vs Change management `★★★`

> [!tip] Case study：项目交付了 ERP，但员工继续用 Excel（shadow IT）——这是 change management 失败。

| | Project Management | Change Management |
|--|-------------------|------------------|
| focus | technical delivery（scope/time/cost/quality） | people adoption（engagement/mindset/behaviours） |
| goal | deliver the solution | deliver **adoption** of the solution |
| success | on time/on budget/to scope | are people using it? confident/competent/committed? |

**Useful phrase**

- *True success comes when end-users adopt the new system and integrate it into daily work to deliver business value.*

---

### Change 三状态 + Kubler-Ross curve `★★★`

**三状态**：current state → transition state（messy middle）→ future state

**Kubler-Ross stages（课件场景化）**

- shock/denial（provide clear info why change）
- anger（listening, empathy, safe channels）
- bargaining（clarify non-negotiables, small flexibilities）
- depression（training/coaching/peer support, quick wins）
- acceptance（reinforce, celebrate, embed into culture）

**Useful phrase**

- *The transition state is the riskiest: confusion, mistakes, and productivity dip.*

---

### 心理与阻力：常见反应 `★★`

Fear of the unknown / loss aversion / cognitive dissonance / status quo bias / social proof  
Diffusion of innovation：early adopters / majority / laggards

---

### OCM 原则与实践策略 `★★★`

**Core principles（课件）**

- clarity of purpose（communicate the why）
- leadership commitment（active sponsorship）
- stakeholder engagement（involve early）
- structured process（ADKAR/Kotter）
- continuous support（training, coaching, feedback channels）

**Practical strategies（课件）**

- communication plan（tailored messaging, multi-channel）
- stakeholder mapping & resistance management（high influence, low support）
- quick wins & iterative delivery（pilots + feedback）
- change agents network（respected staff → peer influence）

**Outcomes**

- business：productivity, customer satisfaction, employee engagement, innovation readiness
- IT：adoption rates, system utilisation, process compliance, governance alignment（ITIL change enablement）

---

### 三大模型：McKinsey 7S / ADKAR / Kotter 8 steps `★★★`

**McKinsey 7S（holistic alignment）**

- Hard：strategy / structure / systems
- Soft：shared values（central）/ skills / style / staff

**ADKAR（individual change）**

- Awareness → Desire → Knowledge → Ability → Reinforcement

**Kotter（top-down momentum）**

1. urgency
2. guiding coalition
3. vision & strategy
4. communicate vision
5. empower action
6. short-term wins
7. consolidate gains
8. anchor in culture

**Useful phrase**

- *ADKAR helps diagnose resistance by locating which stage (awareness, desire, knowledge, ability, reinforcement) is missing.*
- *Kotter’s model sustains momentum through urgency, quick wins, and anchoring change in culture.*
