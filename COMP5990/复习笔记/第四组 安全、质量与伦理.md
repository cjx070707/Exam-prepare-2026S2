## Part 1 — Security basics（W8）

### Security 定义与三维度 `★★★`

**Security（课件）**：保护 information systems（hardware/software/networks/data）免受 unauthorised access, misuse, disruption, destruction。

**三维度**

- **Physical security**：保护基础设施，防盗/破坏/物理篡改
- **Digital/Information security**：保护数据与数字系统，目标 CIA
- **Operational security**：通过流程/人员/政策降低风险（human error、insider threat、weak processes）

**Term**：unauthorised access / misuse / disruption / destruction / operational security (OPSEC)

**Useful phrase**

- *Security protects hardware, software, networks, and data from unauthorised access, misuse, disruption, or destruction.*

---

### Information Security（InfoSec）与 CIA triad `★★★`

**InfoSec**：保护任何形式的信息（digital/physical/spoken）免受未授权 access/use/disclosure/disruption/modification/destruction。

**CIA**

- **Confidentiality**：only authorised access
- **Integrity**：accurate/reliable; not altered improperly
- **Availability**：accessible when needed

**Useful phrase**

- *At its core, information security ensures confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data.*

---

### Vulnerability factors（Human/Tech/Org/Process/Physical） `★★★`

**Human**

- weak passwords / reuse
- phishing/social engineering
- lack of awareness/training
- insider threats (malicious or negligent)

**Technological**

- unpatched/outdated systems
- misconfigurations (firewalls/cloud storage/databases)
- weak/no encryption
- zero-day vulnerabilities

**Organisational**

- lack of policies/governance
- poor incident response planning
- insufficient monitoring/auditing
- third-party vendors without vetting

**Physical/Environmental**

- insecure server rooms
- theft/tampering
- natural disasters; power failures; lack redundancy

**Process/Operational**

- inadequate access control (excessive privileges)
- poor patch/change management

**课件强调**：biggest security threat often = **users**（humans as weakest link）。

**Useful phrase**

- *Humans are often the weakest link; even strong controls can be bypassed through mistakes or carelessness.*

---

### Cybercrimes 典型类型（选背） `★★`

Identity theft & fraud / harassment & cyberbullying / hacking & unauthorised access / data breaches / malware / phishing & social engineering / DDoS / APTs

**Term（常考对比）**

- phishing vs social engineering
- DDoS vs APT

---

### Threat categories & organisational challenges `★★`

Threat categories：human-based / technical / physical / operational / external  
Security hard factors：rapid tech change, sophistication, human factors, complexity, compliance pressure, resource constraints, evolving threats.

---

### Individual rights of data `★★`

（W8 提到，W9–10 的 GDPR 会系统化）

right to be informed / access / rectification / erasure / restrict processing / data portability / rights related to automated decision making and profiling

## Part 2 — Data lifecycle & Security governance（W9–W10）

### Data lifecycle 6–7 stages + security focus `★★★`

> [!tip] 写答案时要能把“控制”放到 lifecycle 每一环：minimisation → encryption → audit logs → masking → retention → destruction。

典型 lifecycle（课件）：creation/collection → storage → usage/processing → sharing/distribution → archival → destruction/disposal

| 阶段 | 安全关注点（课件） |
|------|-------------------|
| collection | minimization；input validation；informed consent |
| storage | encryption at rest；access control；backups |
| processing | encryption in use；RBAC；audit logs |
| sharing | encryption in transit (TLS/VPN)；masking/anonymization；contracts |
| archival | secure long-term storage；retrieval controls；retention schedules |
| destruction | wiping/overwriting；cryptographic erasure；secure disposal |

**Useful phrase**

- *Security controls must be applied at every stage of the data lifecycle; breaches often occur when “inactive” or archived data is forgotten.*

---

### NIST CSF（Identify/Protect/Detect/Respond/Recover） `★★★`

**目的**：risk-based approach；common language；prioritise investments；continuous improvement

**Core functions**

- Identify / Protect / Detect / Respond / Recover

**Implementation tiers**

- Tier 1 Partial（ad hoc, reactive）
- Tier 2 Risk-informed（policies exist but inconsistent）
- Tier 3 Repeatable（documented, consistent, monitored）
- Tier 4 Adaptive（integrated, continuously improved）

**Profiles**：按 business needs & risk tolerance 定制对齐。

**Useful phrase**

- *NIST CSF provides a risk-based approach using five functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.*

---

### GDPR 目的、权利与原则 `★★★`

**目的**：protect personal data & privacy rights；standardise laws；give individuals control

**Individual rights（课件）**：access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, portability, object; right not to be subject to automated decision-making that significantly affects them.

**Key principles**

- lawfulness, fairness, transparency
- purpose limitation
- data minimization
- accuracy
- storage limitation
- integrity & confidentiality
- accountability（demonstrate compliance）

**Useful phrase**

- *GDPR requires organisations to demonstrate accountability and comply with principles such as data minimisation and purpose limitation.*

---

### Cybersecurity domains & practices `★★`

Domains：network / application / information / operational / endpoint / cloud / IAM  
Practices：MFA、patching、firewalls/VPN/IDS、encryption（in transit & at rest）、training、incident response plan、monitor & audit logs.

---

### Standards：ISO 27001 / PCI DSS / HIPAA（高频名词） `★★`

（详细内容在课件里以“为什么重要 + 关键要求/组件”出现，考试通常点名即可。）

- **ISO/IEC 27001**：ISMS；risk assessment + controls + continuous improvement + management involvement；目标 CIA + compliance
- **PCI DSS**：payment card data；requirements：secure network, protect cardholder data, vulnerability program, access control, monitor/test, security policy
- **HIPAA Security Rule**：ePHI；administrative/physical/technical safeguards；penalties & trust impact

## Part 3 — Threat modelling & Incident response（W9–W10）

### Threat modelling 定义 `★★`

结构化识别、理解、优先级排序 threats；提前预判攻击；改进 design/architecture。

---

### STRIDE `★★★`

Spoofing / Tampering / Repudiation / Information disclosure / Denial of service / Elevation of privilege

**Useful phrase**

- *STRIDE is a systematic framework to identify threats across spoofing, tampering, repudiation, information disclosure, denial of service, and elevation of privilege.*

---

### IRP lifecycle `★★★`

Preparation → Identification → Containment（short-term / long-term）→ Eradication → Recovery → Lessons learned

**Useful phrase**

- *Incident response focuses on detecting, containing, and mitigating incidents to minimise damage and restore operations quickly.*

---

### DRP vs IRP vs BCP `★★★`

| | IRP | DRP | BCP |
|--|-----|-----|-----|
| focus | detect/contain/mitigate cyber incidents | recover IT systems/data/operations after disruption | maintain essential business operations during/after disruption |
| timing | during/immediately after incident | post-disaster recovery | proactive + during + after |
| objective | minimise damage | minimise downtime & data loss | maintain critical functions; minimise overall impact |

**Useful phrase**

- *IRP limits damage, DRP restores systems, and BCP keeps critical business functions running; they should be aligned as a complete resilience strategy.*

---

### DR strategies（cold/warm/hot/cloud） `★★`

Cold site / Warm site / Hot site / Cloud DR (DRaaS) / Backup & restore

---

### BCP lifecycle `★★`

project initiation → BIA → risk assessment → strategy development → plan development → testing/exercises → maintenance & continuous improvement

## Part 5 — Quality Assurance & Testing（W11）

### Quality dimensions + cost of poor quality `★★★`

**Quality in IT**：满足 stakeholder needs/expectations，且可靠、安全、可维护。

**Dimensions**：functionality / reliability / usability / efficiency / maintainability / portability / security

**Cost of poor quality**

- direct：bug fixes, downtime, data loss
- indirect：reputation, client loss, legal issues
- failures：Ariane 5（\$370M）、Knight Capital（\$440M）

---

### QA vs QC vs Testing `★★★`

| 概念 | 定位 | 关键词 |
|------|------|--------|
| **QA** | proactive, process-oriented | prevent defects; standards & processes |
| **QC** | reactive, product-oriented | detect defects; verify output |
| **Testing** | QC 的子集 | verification + validation; find defects; reduce risk |

**Useful phrase**

- *QA prevents defects by improving processes; QC detects defects in deliverables; testing is a subset of QC focused on verification and validation.*

---

### QA standards & governance（ISO 9000 / ISO 27001 / ITIL/COBIT/CMMI） `★★★`

- **ISO 9000**：customer focus, leadership, engagement, process approach, improvement, evidence-based decision making, relationship management
- **ISO 27001**：ISMS；risk assessment；114 controls/14 categories；continuous improvement；management commitment
- QA + governance：QA 提供标准与方法；governance 提供 oversight/accountability，确保 QA 不是 ad hoc
- Frameworks：ITIL / COBIT / CMMI

---

### PDCA & CMMI maturity levels `★★★`

**PDCA (Deming cycle)**：Plan → Do → Check → Act（continuous improvement）

**CMMI**：process maturity roadmap；与 QA/PDCA 配合

Levels（课件）

- L1 ad hoc/chaotic
- L2 basic Plan/Do at project level; limited Check/Act
- L3 standardised & documented; consistent Plan/Do; some Check/Act
- L4 statistically measured/controlled; data-driven (Check emphasis)
- L5 proactive continuous improvement/innovation (Act emphasis)

---

### Quality audits（internal/external） `★★`

Quality audit：systematic, independent exam，验证 compliance 与有效性。

- internal (first-party)
- external (second/third-party)
- process / product / system audits

---

### STLC phases + test artifacts `★★★`

**STLC**：parallel to SDLC；shift-left；iterative per sprint/release。

Phases（课件表）

- requirements → planning → development (test cases/data) → environment setup → execution → defect tracking → test cycle closure (exit criteria, coverage, TSR)

Artifacts（重点）

- **test plan**（scope/approach/objectives/resources/schedule/roles/risks/tools）
- **test case**（ID, preconditions, steps, expected/actual, status）
- **test data**（valid/invalid/boundary/large volume）

**Useful phrase**

- *Test artifacts provide traceability between requirements and tests and support accountability and compliance.*

---

### Testing types（functional vs non-functional） `★★★`

Functional：unit / integration / system / UAT（alpha/beta）；black-box (BVA, equivalence partitioning)；white-box (coverage)

Non-functional：load / stress / smoke / A/B（课件给了定义与例子）

**Useful phrase**

- *Functional testing verifies what the system does; non-functional testing evaluates quality attributes such as performance, reliability, usability, and security.*

---

### QA across Waterfall/Agile/DevOps `★★`

- Waterfall：phase-based V&V；testing phase 末端集中
- Agile：QA integrated；acceptance criteria；continuous testing; automation
- DevOps：QA embedded in CI/CD；automation + monitoring + feedback

Professional risk（课件表）：late defect discovery / missing tests in fast iterations / over-reliance on automation

## Part 6 — Ethics（W12）

### Ethics 定义 + Laws vs Ethics `★★★`

**Ethics**：right/wrong 的框架；专业 IT 中是影响 privacy/safety/trust 时的 responsible decision-making。  
**Legal compliance alone isn’t enough**：laws lag behind technology。

**Laws vs Ethics（课件表）**

- laws：minimum acceptable standard; formal, enforceable
- ethics：ideal/aspirational; dynamic/contextual; conscience + professional values

---

### Core ethical principles in IT `★★★`

Integrity / confidentiality & privacy / fairness & non-discrimination / accountability & responsibility / transparency & honesty / respect for intellectual property

---

### ACS Code of Ethics（6 values） `★★★`

Primacy of the public interest / Enhancement of quality of life / Honesty / Competence / Professional development / Professionalism

---

### Ethical issues in IT（privacy/IP/cyber/AI/workplace） `★★★`

**Privacy & data protection**：informed consent? indefinite storage? who accountable for breach?  
**IP**：AI-generated mimic? ChatGPT in assignments?  
**Cybersecurity responsibilities**：exploit vuln to teach lesson? duty to disclose unsafe systems?  
**AI & automation**：accountability; life-and-death decisions; bias  
**Workplace**：cut corners on testing? report unethical superior?

---

### Professional code: ethics vs professional conduct `★★`

ACS code of ethics（high-level aspirational）vs code of professional conduct（enforceable rules）

Personal vs professional ethics：冲突时以 professional obligations 为底线并说明理由。

---

### Ethical decision making models `★★★`

- **Consequentialism (utilitarianism)**：outcome-oriented; maximise good; impartiality；限制：难预测、可能 ends justify means
- **Deontological (duty-based)**：rule/duty oriented; intent matters；限制：rigid；duties clash
- **Virtue ethics**：character/virtues; practical wisdom；限制：less prescriptive, subjective

**Useful phrase**

- *Consequentialism judges actions by outcomes; deontology focuses on duties and rules; virtue ethics focuses on professional character and integrity.*

---

### Emerging tech ethics + guiding questions `★★`

AI / big data / IoT / cloud / deepfakes：bias, transparency, accountability, privacy, surveillance, sovereignty, consent, trust erosion。

Guiding questions（课件）

- who benefits / who harmed?
- fairness/privacy/consent issues?
- comply with professional codes & laws?
- long-term societal consequences acceptable?
