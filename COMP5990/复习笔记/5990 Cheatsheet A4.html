Block E · 安全句库 Q8–Q11

At its core, information security ensures confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data.
→ 信息安全核心是 C/I/A
Implement secure password hashing (bcrypt/PBKDF2/Argon2 + unique salts) so credentials remain protected even if the database is compromised.
→ 哈希+盐，库泄露也难还原密码
Use input validation, output sanitization, and parameterized queries to reduce injection and XSS risks.
→ 输入验证、输出净化、参数化查询防注入/XSS

Block E · 应急 & Impact 句库

This requires incident response to detect, contain, and investigate the active security event before damage spreads.
→ 需 IR：检测、遏制、调查进行中事件
This requires disaster recovery to restore encrypted systems and critical services within acceptable RTO/RPO targets.
→ 需 DR：在 RTO/RPO 内恢复系统
A formal incident response plan defines roles, escalation, containment steps, and is validated through exercises.
→ 正式 IR 计划：角色/升级/遏制 + 演练
Maintain secure, isolated backups (offline/immutable, regularly tested) to improve recovery reliability.
→ 离线/不可变备份 + 定期测试
Customers face financial losses and loss of trust; employees bear remediation workload; the company risks reputational damage and regulatory penalties.
→ Impact 三连：客户/员工/公司
IT professionals must place the primacy of the public interest above personal and sectional interests.
→ ACS：公众利益优先 primacy of the public interest
QA prevents defects by improving processes; QC detects defects in deliverables; testing verifies and validates behaviour.
→ QA 防 · QC 查 · Testing 验
